The orange circular emblem is extracted from the supplied wordmark with its original paths, transforms and colors; the viewBox is cropped to the emblem. Bitcoin.org credits the Bitcoin Project and publishes its site under the MIT license (https://github.com/bitcoin-dot-org/Bitcoin.org/blob/master/LICENSE).

Both files are passive, self-contained SVGs with no scripts, external resources or embedded raster images. Render locally, without recoloring or distortion.
